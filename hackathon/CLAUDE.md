# YAID Hackathon

This folder holds a build from a YAID hackathon. The person working here is new to building with Claude Code. YAID hosts are in the room or on the call, but they cannot reach everyone at once.

- When they say they are stuck, or a popup, sign-in, connector, file, data problem, usage limit or error gets in their way, use the walk-me-through skill and its Stuck page.
- When they paste a use case ("Walk me through this: ...") or ask to be walked through a build, use the walk-me-through skill.
- When they come back to carry on ("pick up where I left off"), use the walk-me-through skill. Its progress note is in .yaid-walkthrough/progress.md in this folder.
- Otherwise, help them the normal way. Do not turn every request into a lesson.
